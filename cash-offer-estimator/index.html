Why a Real Number Beats a Guess

Anyone can throw out a lowball number to see if you'll bite. Here's how a legitimate estimate actually works.

The ARV math

Cash offers start from after-repair value

A cash buyer works backward from what your home would sell for fully repaired, then subtracts repair costs, holding costs, and a fair margin for taking on the risk. That's why the estimator asks for value and repair level, not just what you think it's worth today.

The payoff factor

Your mortgage balance comes off the top

The estimate shows the offer range on the property itself. What actually lands in your pocket is that range minus whatever you still owe. If you're not sure of your exact payoff, a rough number still gets you a useful estimate.

The walkthrough gap

A calculator can't see your house

The tool gives you a defensible range in seconds. A short walkthrough lets us verify condition instead of guessing — which is usually what moves your final number toward the top of the range, not the bottom.
